# Data licence

- **Quote texts** in `data/quotes/` are taken word for word from works in the public domain. Every
  source's author and translator died more than 70 years ago, so the texts are free to use in the
  EU, the United States and most other countries. `npm run data:validate` enforces this rule.
- **Community sayings** in `data/community/` are original work by their contributors, dedicated to
  the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Everything else in `data/`** (selection, ids, tags, locators, translations written for this
  project and the JSON Schemas) is dedicated to the public domain under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The digital editions come from [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org), whose own
contributions are also released under CC0, and from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org).
Only the public-domain texts are reused; no Project Gutenberg files or trademarks are
redistributed.
